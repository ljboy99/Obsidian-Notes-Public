A network controller works to connect a business with a network infrastructure. They help to manage all the function and features on a network including security, quality of service and infrastructure

Network controllers maintain a list of network devices and statuses. They also analyze and automate network ops. 

Network controllers can help by detecting issues before they happen, including security threats and changes to infrastructure
.
A network controller acts as a central control point in an [[intent-based network]]
