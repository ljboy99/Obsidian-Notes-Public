Layer 2 switches operate across layer 2, or the Data Link layer of of the OSI model. 
Data Link is also layer 2 of TCP/IP

Layer 2 switches forward based on MAC addresses

It's relatively easy to split up hosts on a network which utilizes Layer 2 switches

This means each port automatically becomes apart of it's own collision domain, as opposed to keeping the entire switch as a collision domain which can disrupt the operations of everything connected to that switch. 

Layer 2 switches are faster than routers or layer 3 switches.
